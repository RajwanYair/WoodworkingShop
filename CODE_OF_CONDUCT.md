# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Cabinet Planner to be a welcoming, respectful project for people with
different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints. Everyone
participating in project spaces is expected to help keep them constructive and
free from harassment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Offer actionable feedback about ideas and work; do not attack people.
- Respect differing opinions and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep communication professional and focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted
sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Do not report conduct concerns in public issues or discussions. The
maintainer will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible,
and may edit or remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect
the community.

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces when someone is representing Cabinet Planner or its community.
